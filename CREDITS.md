# Credits

## Audio

Real recordings are imported by `tools/import_real_sfx.py`; the synthesized
fallbacks (UI, ambience, birds, land, explosion) are generated by
`tools/gen_sfx.py`.

### Gunshot Sounds

- Author: **Vincent Sevedge**
- Source: <https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds>
- License: **CC BY 3.0** — <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/>
- Used for: `shot_rifle*`, `shot_smg*`, `shot_sniper*`, `shot_shotgun*`
  (CZ-52, Mosin, SKS and shotgun recordings, trimmed and normalized)
